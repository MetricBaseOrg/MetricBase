MetricBase is a data-first content brand covering energy markets, crypto, and Indonesian equities. Three verticals. One standard: original analysis, honest framing, no bags to shill.

Why MetricBase exists

Most financial media is incentive-misaligned.

Sponsored token reviews. Paid "analysis" dressed as editorial. Traffic-optimised headlines that bury the actual signal. We started MetricBase because we got tired of reading it.

The premise is simple: data-driven research published with honest framing should be the baseline, not the exception. That's what every article, chart, and weekly brief on this network is built around.

We cover three verticals — energy, crypto, Indonesian equities — because each one intersects with the others in ways mainstream coverage misses. A crude oil supply shock touches BTC miners. An IDX sector rotation reflects global macro. We read across all three, so our readers don't have to.

What we are not
Not a financial advisor
Nothing we publish is personal investment advice. See our Disclaimer.
Not pay-to-play
We do not accept payment to cover specific assets. Sponsored content, where it exists, is clearly labeled inside the article.
Not a trading signal service
We publish analysis and context. How you trade on it is your decision, your risk.

01 Data before narrative Every claim is anchored to publicly verifiable data — EIA, OPEC, on-chain explorers, exchange APIs, or official IDX filings. We cite our sources. Always.
02 Risk is always disclosed We cover YMYL topics. Every trading-related article carries a prominent risk disclaimer, not buried in the footer, before the article body.
03 Original work only Republished press releases, summarised Twitter threads, and "hot take" rewrites are not MetricBase content. Every piece adds something that isn't already out there.
04 Corrections are public If we publish something wrong, we correct it with a visible note on the article. No quiet edits. No deletions to bury errors. The record is the record.
05 No bags to shill Authors disclose any position in covered assets. No payment to feature specific tokens, stocks, or projects. If coverage feels promotional, send us a correction request.
06 Named accountability Every article carries a named byline. The author is reachable. Anonymous commentary dressed as analysis is not how we operate.

On the use of AI tools: Articles on MetricBase may be drafted with the assistance of AI writing tools. Every published piece is reviewed, edited, fact-checked against primary sources, and finalized by a named human author before publication. AI-generated text that we cannot verify is not published.

We treat AI as a drafting and research assistant, not as an author. The analysis, the sourcing decisions, and the editorial judgment on any given article are human. If you spot a factual error — AI-related or otherwise — use the editorial correction channel and we will investigate.
