SECTION 01

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site operators about how the site is being used.

This policy covers cookies and similar tracking technologies (including local storage, pixel tags, and web beacons) used across MetricBase properties:

  • metricbase.org — the root portal and Journal
  • energy.metricbase.org — Energy Markets vertical
  • chain.metricbase.org — Crypto & Technology vertical
  • saham.metricbase.org — Pasar Saham Indonesia vertical
  • subs.metricbase.org — newsletter subscription

This Cookie Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides broader context about how we handle personal data.

SECTION 04

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our properties — which articles get read, what traffic sources drive visits, and where people leave. This data is aggregated and used solely to improve editorial content and site performance.

Analytics cookies are only set after you accept the cookie consent banner. If you decline or close the banner without accepting, no analytics cookies will be placed.

Cookie name Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique users by assigning a randomly generated number as a client identifier. 2 years
_gid Google Analytics Distinguishes users within a shorter session window. Used to throttle request rates. 24 hours
_ga_* Google Analytics 4 Maintains session state for GA4 measurement ID(s) associated with MetricBase properties. 2 years

Google Analytics data is processed under Google's privacy infrastructure. IP addresses are anonymized by default in GA4. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party cookies

Some pages on MetricBase properties embed content or tools from third-party services. These services may set their own cookies, which are governed by their respective privacy policies. Third-party cookies we are aware of include:

  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log your IP address for CDN routing purposes.

  • Kit.com / subs.metricbase.org — the newsletter subscription form may set cookies related to form validation and email delivery preferences.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party services for full details on their data practices.

SECTION 07

Managing cookies

7.1 — Cookie consent banner

When you first visit metricbase.org, a cookie consent banner is displayed. You can:

  • Accept all cookies — enables essential, analytics, and advertising cookies
  • Decline / close — only essential cookies will be set

Your choice is saved for 365 days. You can reset it by clearing your browser cookies for metricbase.org.

7.2 — Browser cookie controls

Every major browser allows you to view, manage, block, or delete cookies. Links to instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome — Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari — Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge — Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Note: blocking all cookies will prevent certain features (including analytics and ads) but should not break core content on most MetricBase pages.

7.3 — Google Analytics opt-out

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7.4 — Google ad personalization

To control how Google uses your data for ad personalization, visit My Ad Center. You can opt out of interest-based advertising while still seeing non-personalized ads.

SECTION 08

Do Not Track

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal to websites. Currently there is no universally agreed-upon standard for how websites must respond to DNT signals. MetricBase does not alter cookie behavior based on DNT signals at this time. We will revisit this position if a binding standard is adopted.

For effective opt-out, we recommend using the browser controls and opt-out tools described in Section 7.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our properties after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
