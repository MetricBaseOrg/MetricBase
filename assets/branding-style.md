# Brand Style

## Brand

MetricBase

### Tagline (max 6 words)

“Precision growth. No wasted motion.”

### Typography

- bold, modern sans-serif
- “Metric” in white, “Base” in gold
- sharp, clean spacing

Use the provided `MetricBase.png` logo or `bun-character-design-sheet.png` as the core visual DNA. Every design decision must strictly derive from the logo’s or main character's identity.

---

## Main Character

### Basic Info

- **Name:** Bun
- **Species:** Anthropomorphic Penguin (cute, chibi style)
- **Role:** Retail Trader / Market Reactor
- **Archetype:** The Emotional Trader — easily shocked, curious, driven by FOMO but always optimistic

---

### Visual Signature

- **Body Color:** Brown & white
- **Hair:** Small manbun (unique and recognizable trait)
- **Glasses:** White-frame 3D glasses
  - Right lens: Red
  - Left lens: Blue
- **Default Expression:** Big expressive eyes, often shocked or overreacting

---

### Personality

- Highly reactive to market movements (pump → euphoria, dump → panic)
- Curious and always chasing the latest trends
- Relatable to retail traders (stocks, crypto, airdrops, etc.)
- Slightly naive, but acts as an entry point for education

---

## DO / DON’T SECTION

**DO:**

- Show real metrics
- Remove noise
- Prioritize clarity

**DON’T:**

- Overhype
- Follow trends blindly
- Add unnecessary complexity
- React emotionally

---

## Visual Style

- Premium fintech aesthetic
- Sharp linework, not messy
- Subtle glow effects (gold only)
- Structured layout (grid-based chaos, but controlled)

---

## Color Rules

STRICT palette:

- Deep black background (#0a0a0a)
- Gold accents (#c9a84c variations)
- White for contrast
- NO additional colors except subtle gray tones

---

## Depth & Detail

Include:

- 20–40 visual elements total
- arrows, micro-charts, UI panels
- speech bubbles with sharp tails
- small floating numbers/data points
- layered composition (foreground/mid/background)

---

## Final Feel

- like a Bloomberg terminal turned into a person
- like a strategist, not a marketer
- intelligent, composed, slightly intimidating
- visually dense, no empty space
- something worth studying, not just viewing
