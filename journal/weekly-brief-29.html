Weekly Brief — #29 · Week of November 16, 2026

Bitcoin Falls 9.9% to $68,258, the JCI Jumps 5.8% to 5,344 After MSCI, and Brent Slips to $95.84 — What the Data Says

A hot October CPI pushed the US 10-year above 5.1% and put a December Fed hike fully in the price. Bitcoin lost 9.9% to $68,258 and broke $70k for the first time since August. The JCI rallied 5.82% to 5,344 after a mild MSCI review, while the rupiah slid to 18,206. Brent settled at $95.84 for a second weekly loss.

01 Energy Markets Energy

Last week we asked whether Brent would hold $98. It did not. January Brent settled at $95.84 on Friday, up 2 cents on the day and down about 2.5% from $98.32. December WTI settled at $88.56, down $2.27 or 2.5% from $90.83. That is a second straight weekly loss for both grades, but a far smaller one than the 13% drop the week before.

The deal bet neither unwound nor paid off. Iran still wants the blockade and sanctions lifted before talks begin. On Thursday Trump said a deal was not needed and the blockade stays, and prices first rose, then slipped. On Friday the IEA said the market is returning to surplus as Gulf exports recover, with fourth-quarter supply about 1.7 million barrels a day above demand. The Brent–WTI gap narrowed to $7.28 on mixed contract months, from about $7.50.

The October Indonesian Crude Price we have been waiting for is out: $117.24 a barrel, up $4.43 from September's $112.81. The government projects November at $103.94 to $108.94. ICP lags the futures market by a month, so the October print still carries the war premium that Brent has now shed.

Brent fell 2.5% to $95.84 and WTI 2.5% to $88.56, while the IEA called a return to surplus. The war premium is no longer being paid out in one shot. It is bleeding, and with a stalemate on the blockade and supply coming back, the path of least resistance is still lower.

02 Crypto & Technology Crypto

Last week we asked whether BTC would hold the $74,770 Friday low or reclaim $80k. Neither. From Monday on, BTC never traded above $75,523, and it closed below $74,770 on Tuesday, CPI day. It finished Friday at $68,258, down 9.9% from $75,760, after a 4.4% Friday drop that took it below $70k for the first time since August. The Friday low was $67,527. ETH fell 13.8% to $1,928, and ETH/BTC slipped to about 0.0283 from 0.0295.

The trigger was rates. October CPI rose 0.4% on the month against a 0.3% forecast, with core at 0.3% against 0.2%. The 10-year rose to about 5.14% on Friday, up roughly 30 basis points on the week. FedWatch now prices a December 9 hike as a certainty, with about 13% to 14% odds of 50 basis points. Spot Bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.07 billion on the week, per Farside data cited in reports.

Stocks did not follow crypto down. The Nasdaq rose 2.27% to 25,136.27 and the S&P 500 2.51% to 7,561.67, ending a four-week losing streak. The Nasdaq held the 24,579 level we flagged. The damage carried into the weekend: Sunday's partial candle touched $61,961, inside the $62,000 to $66,000 band where Glassnode says heavy buying took place.

BTC fell 9.9% to $68,258 and ETH 13.8% to $1,928 while the Nasdaq rose 2.27%. This was not a tech selloff dragging crypto. It was a rates repricing, and Bitcoin traded as the most rate-sensitive asset on the board.

03 Pasar Saham Indonesia IDX

Last week we said everything hung on the MSCI decision. It turned out to be the least bad outcome. The November review removed Barito Pacific (BRPT) and Bumi Resources Minerals (BRMS) from the MSCI Global Standard Indexes, added no Indonesian names and did not change the country's status. The changes take effect after the November 30 close. The classification decision is due by June 30, 2027.

Relief was immediate. The JCI rose 2.66% to 5,250.89 on Thursday and 1.78% to 5,344.41 on Friday, for a weekly gain of 5.82% from 5,050.43. It held both 5,000 and the 4,904 low. Average daily value more than doubled to Rp23.7 trillion from Rp11.04 trillion. Foreigners were net buyers on the week: Rp463 billion per IDX data cited by Bisnis and Investortrust, Rp1.02 trillion per Kontan. They sold a net Rp230 billion on Friday. The 2026 net sell is now about Rp97.6 trillion.

The rupiah went the other way. Spot closed at 18,206 per dollar per Bloomberg via Kontan, 0.65% weaker on the week. It set a record-weak close of 18,239 on Thursday, so 18,200 has now been broken. JISDOR fixed at 18,218. Cheaper oil did not help this week. A firm dollar and a 5% Treasury yield did the damage. Bank Indonesia meets on November 17–18, with the decision due Wednesday at 14:00 WIB.

The JCI jumped 5.82% to 5,344, foreigners turned net buyers and the rupiah fell to 18,206. Equities priced the removal of a tail risk. The currency priced US rates. Bank Indonesia now has to choose which of the two it is defending.
