Crude Grinds Higher, BTC Leads a Cleaner Leg, IDX Hits Highs on Dovish BI — What the Data Says

01 Energy Markets Energy

Crude is firming again. WTI added 1.7% to close at $83.20, working back toward the top of the $80–84 range it has held since the OPEC reversal, and this time the bid is built on demand rather than positioning. With the meeting noise gone, the market is trading the summer balance — and that balance is tightening. A third consecutive EIA inventory draw and refinery utilization holding above 90% have shifted the near-term picture from 'well-defended floor' to 'grinding toward the ceiling.'

The setup rhymes with three weeks ago, but with a cleaner foundation. Then, the push toward $84 was crowded into a binary OPEC event; now it is a demand-led grind with no comparable catalyst overhanging it. That makes a retest of the $84–86 zone more durable if it comes, because it would not be carrying the same one-sided positioning that made the last breakout fragile. The risk has rotated from event risk to simple overbought-ness — a healthier problem to have.

Henry Hub Gas $2.64

Natural gas extended its run to $2.64/MMBtu, a fresh multi-month high, as a sustained early-summer heat dome over the US South kept cooling demand and power burn elevated. The gas trade that began as a weather divergence a month and a half ago has matured into the cleanest directional energy story we cover. We have flagged it as a relative winner for five straight weeks; at these levels the easy gains are behind it, but the seasonal tailwind runs through the summer and the structural LNG bid is unchanged.

Crude is grinding back toward the top of its range on demand-led strength — a third straight EIA draw and 90%+ refinery runs — not positioning. A retest of $84–86 would be more durable than May's failed breakout because it isn't crowded into an event. The risk is overbought-ness, not a catalyst; respect $84 as the level that defines the next breakout attempt. Gas's summer uptrend is intact but extended.

02 Crypto & Technology Crypto

BTC broke its base to the upside, closing the week at $97,100 — up 3.6% and back above the $94,000 swing line we have tracked for a month, on a convincing weekly close. By our own framework, reclaiming $94,000 reopens the bullish case, and the market took it cleanly. The base-building of the prior two weeks did its job: the leverage was flushed, the weak hands shaken out, and this leg higher is being driven by spot demand stepping back in rather than leverage piling on.

But the character of this advance is different from the May run, and the difference is the whole story. This is a BTC-led move, not an alt-led one: BTC dominance climbed to 56.4%, ETH/BTC slipped to 0.0309, and the Altcoin Season Index sits at 49 — squarely neutral, nowhere near the froth of three weeks ago. Capital is concentrating in the majors and treating BTC as the quality expression of crypto risk, not chasing the long tail. That is a more durable, lower-beta kind of strength, even if it is less exciting for alt holders.

A higher-quality leg — with $100k back in view

Funding has ticked up to +0.011% — warm but not yet hot, and well short of the +0.021% that preceded the May flush. Open interest is rebuilding at a measured pace. As long as this leg stays spot-led and dominance stays firm, the path toward another run at $100,000 is open, and a BTC-led approach to that level is structurally safer than the alt-fuelled one that failed in May. The level to respect on the downside flips to $94,000, now reclaimed support; a weekly close back below it would put the market back into the base.

For local participants the message is to favour quality. With dominance rising and alts lagging, this is not the tape to reach down the risk curve — the rotation-aware playbook from our 2026 research says BTC-led regimes reward concentration in the majors and punish premature alt-chasing. Indonesian venue flow reflects it: net buying has concentrated in BTC, with the speculative alt pairs that ran hot in May still seeing net outflows.

BTC reclaimed $94,000 on a weekly close — by our framework the bull case is back open, and this leg is the higher-quality kind: spot-led, BTC-led, with dominance at 56% and funding warm but not hot. Another run at $100k is in view and structurally safer than May's alt-fuelled attempt. $94,000 flips to support; favour quality (BTC over the alt tail) while dominance rises. A weekly close back below $94k resets to base-building.

03 Pasar Saham Indonesia IDX

The IDX pushed to a fresh 2026 high, closing at 7,470 — up 0.7% — and it got there the way it wanted to: with a home-grown catalyst. Bank Indonesia held its policy rate as expected at midweek, but the statement leaned dovish, explicitly flagging easing external pressure and a stable Rupiah as conditions that open the door to a first cut later in the year. That is the signal the market was waiting for, and it extended the recovery into new-high territory on broad participation.

Crucially, the leadership is now genuinely broad. The banks rallied on the dovish BI tone (lower rates ahead support loan growth and valuations), the commodity names firmed alongside crude's grind higher, and consumer staples held. A market printing new highs with the financials, the cyclicals, and the defensives all contributing is the healthiest configuration we have seen across this seven-week run. The single-sector concentration risk we flagged in May has resolved into genuine breadth.

The Rupiah firms, the cross-vertical aligns — again

USD/IDR firmed to 16,020, its strongest in over two months, as the dovish-but-stable BI message and a still-soft global dollar backdrop drew capital in. A firm Rupiah and a dovish central bank are a powerful combination for Indonesian equities: the first keeps foreign money comfortable, the second promises cheaper domestic funding ahead. For the first time in this series, the IDX's biggest tailwind is at least partly domestically authored rather than purely imported from US data.

Step back and the three verticals are aligned to the upside again — crude grinding higher on summer demand, BTC leading a higher-quality leg toward $100k, and the IDX at new highs on a dovish BI and a firm Rupiah. We have seen this alignment before, in mid-May, and we have seen how quickly it can reverse when the shared US-data nerve gets pinched. The configuration is constructive today; the discipline is to remember that it is a correlation, not a guarantee. A hot US inflation print remains the single event that could turn all three at once.

The IDX hit new 2026 highs on a home-grown catalyst — a dovish BI hold — with the broadest leadership of the entire run: banks, commodities, and consumer all contributing. A firm Rupiah at 16,020 and the promise of cheaper funding ahead are a powerful combination. The three verticals are aligned to the upside again; the configuration is constructive but it is a correlation — watch the next US inflation print as the shared risk that could turn energy, crypto, and the IDX together.
