SECTION 04

Risk warnings

Each of the asset classes we cover carries substantial and category-specific risk. The lists below are not exhaustive.

4.1 — Cryptocurrency & digital assets

Crypto risk

Cryptocurrency and digital asset markets are extremely volatile, largely unregulated in many jurisdictions, and carry substantial risk including:

  • Total loss of capital
  • Loss of access to wallets, private keys, and seed phrases
  • Smart contract exploits and protocol failures
  • Rug pulls, exit scams, and fraudulent projects
  • Regulatory action including outright bans on holdings or trading
  • Exchange insolvency, hacks, or restricted withdrawals
  • Tax obligations that may be unclear, complex, or retroactive
  • Memecoin volatility, including coordinated wash trading and pump-and-dump schemes
  • Custody risk on both centralized and self-custodial platforms

The Indonesian regulatory environment for crypto continues to evolve. Bappebti and OJK have changing jurisdictional authority over digital assets. Verify the current legal status of any crypto activity in your jurisdiction before participating.

4.2 — Equity markets (saham)

Stock market risk

Investing in stocks, including Indonesian equities listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX), carries risk including:

  • Loss of capital up to the full invested amount
  • Liquidity risk (inability to sell at a desired price or at all)
  • Concentration risk if portfolios are not adequately diversified
  • Currency risk for non-IDR investors
  • Corporate actions (rights issues, splits, delistings) that may materially affect valuation
  • Market manipulation by larger participants
  • Macroeconomic and geopolitical shocks
  • Earnings volatility and unexpected disclosures

Past performance of any stock is not indicative of future results. IDX-listed companies are regulated by OJK; their official disclosures via IDXnet are the authoritative source for any investment decision.

4.3 — Energy commodities & derivatives

Energy market risk

Commodities including crude oil, natural gas, refined products, and related derivatives involve substantial risk including:

  • Extreme price volatility driven by geopolitics, OPEC decisions, and supply shocks
  • Leverage that can result in losses exceeding initial deposits
  • Counterparty risk in futures and OTC markets
  • Storage, delivery, and contango/backwardation considerations
  • Regulatory and policy shifts affecting energy markets globally
  • Complex tax treatment of commodity gains and losses
  • Settlement risk in physically-delivered contracts

Trading commodities or derivatives often requires authorization from a regulated broker. Verify your eligibility and regulatory obligations before participating.

4.4 — Leverage and derivatives (all asset classes)

Leverage risk

Margin trading, futures, perpetuals, options, and other leveraged instruments amplify both gains and losses. You can lose more than your initial deposit. Margin calls and forced liquidations are common features of leveraged trading.

Leveraged instruments are unsuitable for most retail participants. If you do not fully understand how leverage affects margin, liquidation prices, funding rates, and tax outcomes, do not use them.
