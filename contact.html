What we can't help with

We are an editorial brand, not a financial advisor or service provider. To set expectations clearly, here are the kinds of messages we cannot respond to in a useful way:

  • "Should I buy/sell [specific asset]?" — We do not provide personalized trading or investment advice. See our Disclaimer for the limits of our editorial product.
  • Portfolio reviews or trade approval — We do not review individual portfolios or sign off on trade decisions.
  • "Can you recover my lost crypto / hacked account?" — We are not a recovery service. Contact your exchange's support, then local authorities. Be wary of recovery scams.
  • Tax or legal advice — Consult a licensed tax professional or lawyer in your jurisdiction.
  • Shill requests for specific projects — We do not accept payment to cover specific assets. Sponsored content, where it exists, is clearly labeled within the article.

Questions in any of these categories will receive a polite redirect to our Disclaimer or to an appropriate qualified professional.
