What you can do

One world. Every system
you'd expect from a real economy.

GATHER & CRAFT
Gather & Craft

Woodcutting, mining, fishing, and farming feed six craft families. Specialize, earn mastery XP, and produce Fine or Master-quality gear.

ECONOMY
Living Economy

NPC shop prices move with real supply and demand. Regional pricing, caravans, adaptive world events, and a player-run gold market keep the economy alive.

MARKETS
Companies & Exchange

Found a company, issue shares, and trade them on the in-game stock exchange. Real capital markets, player-run.

GOVERNANCE
DAO Governance

$BASE holders vote on token-weighted polls that steer the direction of the world.

BUILD
Player Worlds

Buy a zone for 1M gold and build it out with a full in-game editor — rivers, farm plots, crop markets, gatherable nodes. Sell visitor passes and tax what they gather.

PVP
PvP & Territory

Danger-tier zones, loot drops, bounties, guild wars, castle sieges, seasons and ranks. Mounts, pets, and gear that gets rarer the deeper you go.

COMMUNITY
Mail & Community

Send letters with gold attached, run a guild bank, and keep your economy connected across every town.

$BASE
$BASE Utility

Token-gated zones, burn sinks, a 1:1 gold desk, and a live public dashboard tracking every gold flow, burn, and market move in the world.
